Office Tileset (16x16 tiles, 1px spacing)

Original artwork created for the QuotePlatform virtual office.
According to the project owner, the image was produced with AI assistance: a script draws every
tile pixel by pixel. It was not downloaded or copied from WorkAdventure or from any other tileset
pack; the style is only loosely inspired by modern-office tilesets.
